# Coir substrate: permittivity → VWC and pore-water EC (sources and numbers)

Compiled 2026-10-27 for the SAG kernel (currently: Topp 1980 inversion → ε_b; Hilhorst 2000 eq. 14 with ε_0 = 4.1; 2 %/°C to 25 °C; refuse θ < 0.15).

**Rules used in these notes**
- Every number below comes from a source that was opened (full text, manufacturer PDF, or abstract), and the access level is stated. Numbers marked **[computed here]** are arithmetic from cited equations. They are not published values.
- "FT" = full text read; "ABS" = abstract only (or abstract plus metadata); "MAN" = manufacturer document read in full.
- Notation: ε_b = bulk real permittivity, σ_b/ECb = bulk EC, σ_p/ECp = pore-water EC, σ_e/ECe = saturated-extract EC, ε_0 = ε_σb=0 (Hilhorst offset, also called the "soil parameter").

## Q3. Hilhorst ε_0 (ε_σb=0) for coir/organic substrates, validity limits, accuracy

## Conflicts and caveats

1. **ε is instrument-specific in coir. This is the biggest risk for the kernel.** At about 51 % VWC, the GS3 (70 MHz) read ε ≈ 9.2 in Bañón's coir. The WET150-Coir curve implies ε ≈ 24 at θ = 0.51 **[computed]**. Yan's VNA line gives θ > 1 for ε > 28, so it only applies inside its own measured ε range. An ε inferred by inverting Topp from an LSE01 VWC therefore cannot be dropped into another sensor's coir curve, or into Hilhorst, without an LSE01-in-coir calibration. Better options: the LSE01 can output a **raw "Dielectric constant" in MOD=1**, but scaling is undocumented (Dragino LSE01 manual §2.3.2). Or use the manufacturer's 3-point remap (Dragino note).
2. **Direction of the Topp/mineral error is consistent: it underestimates in coir.** The size ranges from ≈ 0.07–0.18 m³/m³ (computed, WET150 curves) to 0.25–0.33 RMSE (ECH2O, van der Westhuizen 2011) and ≈ 17–25 points (Dragino coco example). The kernel's θ < 0.15 refusal is likely to trigger falsely in coir slabs that are actually at about 0.22 or above.
3. **ε_0 for coir conflicts across sources.** Lee & Kim 2024 report 1.64 (abstract only, sensor unnamed). Delta-T and the TEROS 12 manual use 4.1. METER's GS3 manual recommends 6 for growth media. Hilhorst's rockwool value was 4.1. Miyamoto 2021 found the default Hilhorst **overestimates** ECw in coir. A lower ε_0 would *reduce* σ_p (larger denominator), which fits that finding. Even so, Lee & Kim still had RMSE 2.28 dS/m against σ_e. No full-text coir ε_0 with error bars was obtained.
4. **Pore EC ≠ any grower reference.** σ_p (Hilhorst), slab-syringe solution, drain EC, pour-through, SME, 1:1.5 and 1:5 extracts all differ systematically. The published coir conversion (σ_e = 0.5381σ_p + 1.6493) has R² 0.62. The user's premise "1:1.5 = EN 13652" is wrong: EN 13652 is 1:5 v/v (Verhagen 2011), and EN 13038 excludes preformed slabs.
5. **The θ minimum for σ_p depends on the calibration.** A fixed θ = 0.15 is inconsistent with WET150 practice (coir 20.3 %, equivalent to ε_b − ε_0 ≥ ≈ 3, computed). METER uses 0.10. Scoggins recommends ≥ 0.13 to read at all and ≥ 0.35 for comparable values. A permittivity-based gate (ε_b − ε_0 ≥ 3) is calibration-independent; a θ-based gate is not.
6. **Salinity interacts with calibration.** Calibrations made at low EC (Rhie & Kim: coir SME 0.84 dS/m; Yan: desalted coir) will overestimate VWC in fertigated slabs at 2–6 dS/m (Bañón: about 4 points at 5 dS/m and 51 % VWC). Bañón's salinity-specific coir equations need σ_p as an input, which creates circularity with Hilhorst.
7. **Temperature: 2 %/°C is the industry default (Delta-T 2.0; METER 1.9; SigmaProbe 1.7 worked), but it is ionic-composition dependent**, and it failed for the WET at ≥ 3–5 dS/m (Scoggins 2006). No coir-specific nutrient-solution coefficient was found. Hayashi (2004) and other generic natural-water coefficients were *not verified* in this session and are not used here.
8. **Access limits.** Lee & Kim 2024 (CC-BY, but blocked by a bot check), Miyamoto 2021, Incrocci 2009, Balendonck 2005, van der Westhuizen 2011/2013 and Nemali 2007 were seen as **abstracts only**. Rhie & Kim's GS3 "ADC" units are ambiguous. Bañón's EC5 "mV" appear to be volts. The WET-2 coir coefficients (WET-GH-1) are not published in the manual. The Scoggins Table 3 rows were read from PDF text whose layout was garbled; the WET assignment was cross-checked by the slope ordering PT < SME < 1:2.
9. **Dragino documentation conflicts.** The LSE01 is said to be calibrated for "Mineral soil type" (§1.1) and also for "saline-alkali soil and loamy soil" (§4.2). The FDR frequency and EC basis are undocumented. The coco example uses a Bluelab handheld as the reference, not gravimetry.
